Rebound It Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Rebound It is a game for iPhone and Android made by an independent developer, raestio ("I", "me"). This policy explains what data the game uses and who receives it.

What the game itself stores

Your best score and your sound setting are saved only on your device. I don't run any servers, and the game has no accounts, sign-in or analytics of its own. I never receive or sell any of your data.

Ads (Google AdMob)

After a game over you can choose to watch an ad to continue. These ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's software in the game may collect:

  • your IP address, which is used to estimate your approximate location (country or city, not your precise location)
  • device identifiers: on iPhone, the identifier for vendor, and the advertising identifier (IDFA) if you allow tracking; on Android, the advertising ID and app set ID
  • information about the ads you see and how you interact with them, and app launches
  • crash logs and performance data (such as launch time) from the ads software

Google handles this data under its own policy. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Tracking and personalised ads

iPhone: iOS may ask whether Rebound It can track your activity across other companies' apps and websites. If you allow it, Google can use the advertising identifier to show ads that are more relevant to you. If you don't, you still see ads, but they are not personalised by that identifier. You can change your choice at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Android: Google can use your device's advertising ID to show ads that are more relevant to you. You can reset or delete the advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's settings, usually under Settings › Google › Ads or Settings › Privacy › Ads.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change that choice later with Privacy choices on the game's How to play screen.

Leaderboards

If you are signed in to Game Center (iPhone) or Google Play Games (Android), your best score is submitted to the Rebound It leaderboard together with your player profile. Apple handles this under the Apple Privacy Policy, and Google under the Google Privacy Policy. I see only what the leaderboard shows publicly.

Children

Rebound It is not directed at children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect personal data from them.

Your rights

Because I don't hold any of your data, there is nothing for me to access or delete. To control ad-related data, use the choices above or contact Google through its privacy policy. Uninstalling the game removes your saved best score and settings from your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
